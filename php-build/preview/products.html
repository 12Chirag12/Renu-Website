Supported Capsules Types & Formats

6 Specialized Variations
01
Hard Gelatin Capsules (HGC)

Standard pharmaceutical-grade gelatin shells with rapid disintegration in gastric fluid.

02
Vegetarian (HPMC) Capsules

Plant-derived hypromellose shells with low moisture content (<7%), ideal for hygroscopic APIs.

03
Pellets-in-Capsule (MUPS)

Controlled-release micro-pellets and coated beads filled with precise multi-unit dosage accuracy.

04
Delayed / Enteric Capsules

Acid-resistant capsule shells protecting acid-labile molecules without needing solvent coating.

05
Combination Fills

Dual filling capabilities such as tablet-in-capsule or multi-pellet formulations for combination therapy.

06
Banded & Tamper-Evident

Hermetically sealed gelatin or HPMC liquid banding around the joint for tamper evidence and stability.
